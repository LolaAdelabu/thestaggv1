The Stagg is a café built around good food and good values. Every menu item is made with organic, ethically and locally sourced ingredients, celebrating fresh seasonal flavors while supporting local farmers and producers. With a portion of every purchase helping fund charities that combat food insecurity, it's a place where every meal can have a positive impact beyond the table.

Location

Set in a vibrant downtown neighborhood, The Stagg brings people together over locally sourced food in a space designed for conversation and connection.

Address: 2477 Science Center Drive, Shoreville MN,83445
